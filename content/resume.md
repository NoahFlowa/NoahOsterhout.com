## Experience

### Software Developer, [SunFrog Solutions](https://sunfrogsolutions.com)

`2022 to present`

- **Manager 2.0.** Led the full rewrite of SunFrog's merchant platform as an API-first app in Next.js 16, Tailwind CSS 4, and shadcn/ui, replacing the legacy Merchpond Manager in October 2026. The new platform uses a custom merchant architecture, with scoped data mutation and routing that leaves global routes untouched, and merchant role-based access control for create, read, update, delete, and export.
- **[SunFrog.com](https://sunfrog.com).** Maintain the staff back office, the school portal, the artist and seller portal, and integrations for SunFrog's design marketplace (Next.js 16, Tailwind CSS 4, Vercel, Supabase, Cloudflare).
- **AI and MCP services.** Built the AI and MCP services that let customers connect personal AI assistants to SunFrog.com to create and order their own private custom designs without leaving the assistant.
- **Developer platform.** Rebuilt the [SunFrog Developer Docs](https://developer.sunfrogsolutions.com) and rewrote the SunFrog Developer API, extending it for 3PL fulfillment, with multi-carrier shipping (DHL, USPS, UPS, FedEx, Pitney Bowes) and inventory sync with Shopify, Gameday Couture, and Fanatics.
- **Velocity-CID.** Built SunFrog's internal hosting platform for its new-stack Next.js sites on company servers, end to end.
- **Market intelligence.** Built data platforms that gather competitive insight across the print-on-demand industry, including the in-progress HIVE and a free tool that helps customers compare print-on-demand providers.
- **Legacy systems.** Maintained and extended the company-wide back office of internal reports and tools, and upgraded it from Adobe ColdFusion 2011 to ColdFusion 2023. Maintained the Merchpond Manager merchant portal from 2023 until it was retired.
- **Business operations.** Helped migrate company sites toward Supabase, Vercel, Next.js, Docker, and OpenAPI, and built internal tools for order analytics, inventory, customer service, and HR.
